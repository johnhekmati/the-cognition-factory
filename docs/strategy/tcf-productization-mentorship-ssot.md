# TCF Productization Mentorship SSOT

**Status:** Living internal document

**Role of this document:** Council-of-Groks acting as SME mentors for productizing The Cognition Factory under a structured operating model.

Process pattern used (generalized): purpose/strategic alignment → design principles & governance → risk & compliance → KPIs & success metrics → maturity baseline → action plan.  
This pattern is inspired by mature CoE / ISV mentorship structures (e.g. the visible shape of ritewai.com) but is applied strictly to TCF’s own domain: cognitive architecture, dual-lane Adult SaaS + Enterprise readiness, dense maps, Gym/Dojo, and interruptible learning. No Microsoft AI / Fabric / Copilot content is being copied or competed with.

## 2. What Is Not Yet Locked (Gap Analysis)

### Highest-leverage gaps (mentor priority)

**1. Strategic Intent & Precise ICP**  
Who is the first concrete paying Adult buyer? Who is the first Enterprise buyer? What is the non-negotiable wedge versus Khan Academy / MITx OCW / generic tools that makes them pay this month?

**2. Operating Governance**  
Dual-lane firewall rules, content promotion/versioning/quality gates, internal AI usage policy for Practice/side-chat/Gym, claim-review cadence and ownership.

**3. Success Metrics & Instrumentation**  
Adult: time-to-first-map, Save Place return rate, Practice completion/honesty, retention cohorts.  
Enterprise: readiness indicators, gap-closure velocity, hypercare → retainer conversion.

**4. Commercial Packaging & Pricing Architecture**  
Concrete Adult SaaS price anchors, household seats, trial path. Enterprise SOW / implementation / hypercare / retainer structures and rate logic.

**5. Content Factory Operating System**  
Roles, review cadence, SSOT rules, graduation path from learner-house to production, ownership of edge density and cart quality.

**6. Customer Success / Hypercare Playbooks**  
Lightweight Adult success path + full Enterprise implementation → hypercare → retainer transition.

**7. Security, Trust & Compliance Roadmap**  
Access controls, backup/restore, incident response, future SOC 2 trajectory, DPAs for Enterprise — beyond the privacy section of the risk register.

**8. Unit Economics & Capacity Model**  
True cost to acquire, support, and content-maintain one Adult subscriber and one Enterprise engagement. Capacity required before the next core or next Enterprise seat.

**9. Feedback → Product Loop**  
How real usage and Kristi-style calibration systematically flow back into cores, carts, and Gym surfaces.

**10. Later vectors**  
Formal IP operationalization, GTM sequencing & demand generation, explicit partner/channel (or direct-only) decision.
